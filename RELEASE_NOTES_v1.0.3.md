# Screenshot Shelf v1.0.3

Small quality-of-life release focused on launch behavior, screenshot saved popovers, and large preview controls.

## What's changed

- Screenshot Shelf can now open automatically at login, and this is enabled by default.
- Screenshot saved popovers appear faster after taking a screenshot.
- Back-to-back screenshots now stack separate popovers instead of replacing the previous one.
- Popovers pause while hovered and fade out smoothly.
- Press and hold a thumbnail for a quick large-preview peek, then release to close it.
- Click a thumbnail again to close the large preview.
